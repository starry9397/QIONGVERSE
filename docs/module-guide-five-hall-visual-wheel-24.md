# Five Hall Visual Wheel Revision

## Module goal

The `#exhibition` entry is a full-viewport visual gateway to the five Hainan cultural halls. It preserves the existing hall routes and bilingual titles while reducing the visible interface to the active hall name, its sequence, a visual arrow, and a right-edge image wheel.

## Content boundaries

- The five scene images are project-supplied curatorial assets, not official signage, policy material, or factual claims embedded in an image.
- The Free Trade Port remains a separate public-information orientation layer. Its four project exhibits remain in the right-side reading controls; its bottom navigation now enters the five cultural halls.
- No government affiliation, policy approval, visitor eligibility, commercial promise, or new external asset is implied by this revision.

## Interaction

- The full main visual enters the selected hall through the existing route.
- The wheel is a keyboard-accessible tablist. Arrow keys, Home, and End select halls.
- Autoplay advances every two seconds. Hover, keyboard focus, and reduced-motion pause it.
- The wheel uses a 72-degree transform for every selected hall; its image faces stay upright while the track rotates.

## Deliberate visual deviation

This revision replaces the former archive-style copy-led five-hall entry with an image-first full-screen stage, per the user-approved video-reference direction. It retains the project palette, typography, routes, accessibility controls, and source boundaries.

## Acceptance checks

- Each scene image has its existing poster fallback.
- No additional API, browser storage, camera permission, or dependency is introduced.
- The right-side wheel remains compact on mobile rather than becoming a top strip.
- The five cultural hall routes and the Free Trade Port exhibit reading controls continue to work.

## Free Trade Port entry treatment

The five cultural-hall links in the Free Trade Port world use a continuous curatorial label rail. Each link displays a locally supplied scene specimen, hall sequence, bilingual hall name, and entry affordance. Theme accents differentiate the halls only during focus and interaction; they do not make factual claims about the images or turn the navigation into commercial cards.

All homepage and immersive-hall headers use low-opacity theme-tinted glass. This is a deliberate visual refinement to preserve the world behind the navigation while maintaining contrast, focus treatment, and project-specific hall color cues.

The Virtual Exhibition menu begins with a clearly separated `Free Trade Port Main Hall / 自贸港主厅` route. The top-level `Free Trade Port Reading Room / 自贸港阅览室` link scrolls to the existing public-information reading room. This keeps the immersive main hall and source-oriented reading room distinct without asserting government affiliation or endorsement.

The home-page Free Trade Port cover uses a numbered editorial layout rather than a button group. It retains the immersive-main-hall entry and Luoyin orientation entry, removes the redundant five-hall action, and keeps the public-information limitation visibly adjacent to the main action.

## Dawn Home Visual

The homepage uses `qiongverse-hero2.jpg`, a project-supplied brand visual, as its only hero image. Its embedded wordmark is part of the artwork, so the interface uses a concise five-world journey statement rather than repeating the QIONGVERSE name. If the source image cannot load, the hero falls back to a solid deep-sea brand surface while retaining the five-hall and Luoyin actions; it does not substitute another image. This visual treatment is project branding only and does not communicate official endorsement, partnership, tourism availability, or policy information.

## Shared brand lockup

The homepage and all immersive-hall headers share the same transparent project-brand lockup: a star-orbit emblem, `QIONGVERSE` wordmark, and desktop-only `EXPLORE BEYOND. CREATE FOREVER.` strapline. The assets are cropped from the supplied project logo with their original metallic details retained; only the black background was removed. They are project visual assets and do not imply official endorsement, certification, partnership, or other factual claim. At narrower navigation widths the strapline is hidden before controls can collide, while the accessible project name remains available.
